SkillBridge Backup privacy policy

Updated: 12 October 2026

This policy covers the private backup application maintained by ashautomationlab, not the website's customer-facing service.

Information and permissions

The application handles the owner's website backups, which may include configuration, a database, and personal data already contained in that database. Its Google permission is drive.file, allowing access to app-created or explicitly authorized files. It checks storage quota, repository size, and account identity to verify the intended backup account and available space.

Use and storage

Backups are encrypted with Restic before upload to the owner's Google Drive and decrypted locally for recovery checks. The owner controls the encryption password. Authorization and email credentials are stored in protected configuration files and are not published or included in alert messages.

Data is used only for backup, recovery, and operational checks. Alerts contain limited status and storage information, not backup contents or credentials. The application does not sell the data or use it for advertising.

Providers

Google provides backup storage and email delivery; GitHub hosts these information pages. See Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement. These pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, or embedded trackers added by the maintainer.

Retention and access removal

The owner controls retention and deletion. Backup removal uses Restic's supported snapshot and pruning operations after approval; separate offline archives may retain older copies. The owner can revoke the app's Drive access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops access but does not delete existing backups. Email authorization is managed separately.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the information repository. Do not post credentials, backup contents, or other confidential information.
