SkillBridge Backup

SkillBridge Backup is a private application maintained by ashautomationlab to back up and recover the owner's website data. It is not a public backup service.

The application uses Restic to encrypt backups before storing them in Google Drive through Rclone. It verifies restored files, checks storage availability, and emails operational alerts to the owner.

Google Drive access uses the limited drive.file permission: files created by the app or explicitly authorized for use with it. Backup contents and credentials are not published on this website.

Read our privacy policy. For questions, contact the maintainer. Do not post confidential information.
